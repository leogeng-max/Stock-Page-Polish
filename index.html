This page adapts to your position.

01

Alerts

Alerts appear for any security held or under review. They flag order fills, position-level events, and quarterly earnings, as well as delisting notices. A thesis or kill criteria left blank at purchase surfaces here as an outstanding item. The value is not more information. It is knowing which position requires attention, and when.

02

The one liner

The one liner states the thesis in a single sentence, and carries to the active positions screen so every holding shows its case without being opened. The full reasoning stays in the memo. This is the line returned to when checking whether the position still holds. A thesis that cannot be stated in one line cannot be monitored against a price.

03

The memo

The memo records classification, role in the portfolio, thesis, kill criteria, prediction, and source. Thesis and kill criteria can be left blank at purchase and completed later.

Memos are not edited in place. Each revision is issued as a new dated memo, which becomes the current case while every memo before it stays intact and viewable. Nothing is overwritten.

04

The prediction

The prediction records the probability you assign to the thesis, the return hurdle, and the horizon over which you expect it to resolve. It can be revised at any time, and each earlier version stays dated and viewable. What that preserves is the sequence: the claim fixed first, the outcome that settles it second.

05

Notes

Notes keep no history. They can be rewritten at any time, nothing prior is retained, and nothing in them enters the memo. Unsettled thinking requires a surface that keeps no record.

06

Position and returns

The position's return is shown alongside the return the same capital would have produced in the S&P 500 over the same period. The difference isolates what choosing this business added or cost against holding the index instead.

07

Financials

Financials holds the income statement, balance sheet, cash flow, and key ratios, in both quarterly and annual form, alongside the earnings history and the next reporting date. Each figure is shown against the comparable prior period and across the reported history.

Screenshot · Financials card
08

Fiscal.ai

Fiscal.ai holds the primary record of financials, ratios, and filings used across the platform. Any figure surfaced by the AI models below is checked against this record before it enters the case. The distinction matters: a plausible number and a sourced one can look identical until they are tested, and Fiscal.ai is what makes that test possible.

09

AI tools

The links open a working session for building a thesis and testing whether it survives. They accelerate research. They do not replace it. The model can develop the case with you. It cannot own the decision. A conclusion you did not build cannot be defended in a drawdown.

Screenshot · AI tool rows
10

News

Recent headlines for the company are collected here. They give context for movement in the price and are not a substitute for the thesis already on file.

Screenshot · News
11

History

Each order is stamped with the memo in force on its execution date. A past decision can be read against the case that actually produced it. Outcomes change how a decision is remembered, and the record is what holds the original reasoning in place.

12

Capital decision

This control opens the order flow and stays anchored at the bottom of the page. A purchase is placed against a memo already written. A sale closes the position against the memo in force. Transactions are cheap. Outcomes are not.
